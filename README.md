# SABIHA — Digital Writer's Portfolio

> *"WORDS, WRITTEN. STORIES, SHAPED."*

A bespoke, production-quality digital portfolio website for **Sabiha** — Writer, Author, Content Creator, and English Literature graduate.

Designed with the aesthetic sensibility of an independent literary magazine: warm ivory tones, deep charcoal typography, muted wine accents, and subtle antique gold details.

---

## 📖 Highlights & Features

- **Cinematic Typographic Loader**: Letter-by-letter dynamic assembly (`S` → `SA` → `SAB` → `SABI` → `SABIH` → `SABIHA`), identity reveals (*Writer • Creator • Storyteller*), and ethos transition with skip functionality.
- **Interactive Particle Background**: High-performance HTML5 Canvas physics responding to cursor velocity, vector direction, and inertia, with automatic settling and mobile throttling.
- **Published Books Shelf**:
  - *SCREAM* (Author, Mystery Thriller, Notion Press) with verified official cover artwork.
  - *A MUSING MOTLEY* (Co-Author, International Anthology, Amazon).
  - *NIBS OF FIRE* (Compiler, Women's Poetry Collection, Amazon).
- **Authorial Role Triad**: Visual breakdown distinguishing *Author* ("I created the story"), *Co-Author* ("I shared the page"), and *Compiler* ("I brought voices together").
- **Milestone Showcase ("A Moment Worth Remembering")**:
  - Featured editorial spotlight with Sabiha holding her published book *SCREAM*, the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam honor medal, and the Young Author Award certificate presented during the Ezhudhunga 100 authors event in the presence of ISRO scientist Gokul.
- **Professional Content Ecosystem**:
  - Detailed practice overview of work with **Reach Skyline**, covering healthcare content, long-form scripts, short-form reels/shorts, blogs, and Instagram copywriting.
- **Pedagogy & Craft**:
  - English teaching background at **Chaitanya Group of Institutions** highlighting syntax, clarity, audience empathy, and educational storytelling.
- **Behind The Words**:
  - Editorial double-framed portrait container featuring Sabiha's professional photograph.
- **Direct Connect**:
  - Direct `mailto:`, `tel:`, and one-click clipboard copy triggers.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Semantic HTML5** & **Modern CSS3** (Custom Properties, Flexbox, Grid, Fluid Clamped Typography)
- **ES6+ Modular JavaScript** (Zero bloated runtime dependencies)
- **HTML5 Canvas** (Dynamic particle physics engine)
- **Google Fonts** (*Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, *Inter*, *JetBrains Mono*)

## 🚀 Running Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or spin up a local development server:

```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
