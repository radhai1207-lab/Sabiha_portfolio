The Story Behind The Words

Writing was never just a skill.
It became a way of seeing.

Sabiha — Literary & Digital Portfolio

Sabiha holds a BA in English Literature, an academic foundation that grounded her appreciation for syntax, rhythm, critical analysis, and the enduring human impulse to tell stories.

Her professional path began in education as an English teacher at Chaitanya Group of Institutions. In the classroom, she learned how to demystify complex language, command audience attention, and adapt tone to diverse levels of understanding — skills that directly informed her evolution as a writer.

Expanding into professional content creation with Reach Skyline, she brought storytelling rigor to the digital realm. Here, she authored captions, developed narrative content for long-form and short-form videos, and crafted articles and Instagram posts for notable healthcare institutions.

Parallel to her digital work, Sabiha is an independently published author, co-author, and compiler. Her portfolio spans fiction, poetry, healthcare communication, and creative anthologies.
